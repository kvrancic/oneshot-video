# Review checklist (for the fresh-eyes critic)

You see stills from a clip, its edit as text and its plan. You did not make it. Answer each question yes or no; for every no, name the frame or line and the fix. Be specific and short.

## The moment
1. Does the first sentence make sense to a stranger? Does the first frame plus the hook card make them want the next 3 seconds?
2. Does the clip end after its payoff lands, on a finished sentence?
3. Is anything important missing between segments (a qualifier, a negation, a number, the setup of a joke)?
4. Does the on-screen text only say what the speaker said?

## The picture
5. Is the speaker's face clear of captions and graphics in every still?
6. Is the framing steady and the face near the centre (9:16) with headroom, eyes about a third down?
7. Is anything soft enough to notice (a crop upscaled past 2x)?
8. Is any slide cropped, or shown as a washed-out projection where the deck page exists?

8b. Is the first frame a good thumbnail (hook fully drawn, face visible, nothing half-animated)?
8c. In wide shots, does anything on a slide or screen say something the speaker did not (a number, a popup, the answer to a reveal)?

## Text
9. Mixed case, one family plus at most one serif voice switch, two weights?
10. At most one accent word per caption page, on under a fifth of pages?
11. Any misspelled name, wrong number, leftover filler, or broken word ("self- driving")?
12. Everything inside the safe area (x 72 to 1008, y 160 to 1520 on 9:16; nothing under the right-side buttons)?
13. Any text too small to read on a phone (under 26 px on 1080 wide)?

## Motion and sound
14. At most one overlay besides captions at a time; one spent moment in the clip?
15. Something changes every 3 to 6 s, nothing strobes faster than 2 s?
16. Effects 0 to 4 per minute, quiet, none on captions? Music absent or well under the voice?

## Honesty
17. Claims about named people flagged as commentary? Quotes verbatim? Private people unnamed?
18. Would a careful editor at a serious channel call any of it cringe? If yes, what?

Reply with: PASS or FIX, then the numbered issues with the fix for each.
